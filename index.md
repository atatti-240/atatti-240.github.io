### Featured Projects

[StockSpot: Algorithmic Trading System](/stockspot)

ML pipeline that forecasts next-day highs in 13 US cities (R² = 0.95 live) and paper-trades Kalshi weather markets 24/7 on a cloud server I maintain.

---

[BlindSpot: Houston Commute Planner](/blindspot)

2nd place at the Impact Hub Houston Hackathon. Team-built in 48 hours. Predicts train blockages, crash-prone stretches, and congestion to tell Houston commuters when to leave and which way to go, with live AI traffic cameras. [Live demo](https://blind-spot.app/)

---

[Farmers Forward: Farm Tech for Small Farms](/farmers-forward)

Co-founded with friends. Built a model that turns raw NPK, EC, and pH soil readings into guidance small farms in Fort Bend County can act on. Entered in the Congressional App Challenge. [Live app](https://farmersforward-r4ty.onrender.com/)

---

[AIchessbot: UCI Chess Engine (~3100 Elo)](/aichessbot)

C++ engine I wrote from scratch, using bitboards, NNUE evaluation, multi-core Lazy SMP search, and Syzygy endgame tablebases.
